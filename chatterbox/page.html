What it does

Give Chatterbox a short recording of someone speaking and it reads your script in a voice like theirs, picking up their tone and delivery.

Recording a good sample

  • About 10 seconds of one person talking naturally.
  • A quiet room: no music, no background voices.
  • Save it as a .wav file in the samples folder shown below, then reload this page.

Only copy voices you have permission to use

Write your script

Type or paste what you want spoken. Each line becomes its own piece.

One line, one piece

Every line is spoken separately with a pause after it. That means you can fix one line and re-create only that line, without touching the rest.

Punctuation steers the delivery

  • An exclamation mark lifts the energy. A question mark sounds like a question.
  • Commas and ellipses (…) slow things down.
  • A short line on its own reads as a heading and gets a longer pause.

A word comes out wrong?

Spell it the way it sounds ("sequel" for SQL), or add it to overrides.json once and every script gets the fix.

Listen and save

Create the audio, listen, adjust, and save the take you like.

Create audio

Speaks the whole script. It takes about as long as the audio itself. To redo part of it, select those lines in the script and use Create audio for selected lines.

Save this take

Saves the audio as a WAV file, together with the exact settings and text that made it, in a folder named after your take. You can always recreate it later.

    Welcome to Chatterbox Studio

    Chatterbox is a free, open-source voice model from Resemble AI. It runs on this computer, so nothing you type leaves your machine. It works best with an NVIDIA graphics card; without one it still runs, just slowly.

    Your first recording, in four steps

    1. Pick a voice and a style in step 1. Chatterbox's own voice and the Natural style are a good start.
    2. Paste your script in step 2, one sentence or idea per line.
    3. Create audio and listen. The very first time, the studio downloads the model (a few gigabytes), so give it a few minutes.
    4. Happy with it? Name it and Save this take.

    Making it sound better

    • Too flat? Raise Expressiveness. Too theatrical? Lower it.
    • A line came out odd? Select it and create it again with Try a different take.
    • Want your own voice? Record about 10 seconds of yourself and add it as a sample.

    Where your files go

    Saved takes go in the exports folder next to the studio. Each take has the audio, the settings, your script and the text Chatterbox spoke.

    Using the audio

    Chatterbox is released under the MIT license, which allows commercial use. Its audio carries an inaudible watermark marking it as AI-generated. Only copy voices you have permission to use.
