Pick a voice

Use one voice, or blend a few into a voice of your own.

What a blend is

Kokoro comes with a few dozen built-in voices. A blend mixes two or more of them, the way you'd mix paint: half Heart and half Sarah gives a new voice somewhere between the two.

Why you'd blend

  • To sound like you, not like the stock voice everyone else downloads.
  • To soften a quirk. If one voice is a little too bright or breathy, mix in a steadier one.

How to do it

Start with one voice and listen. Then add a second and drag its slider. The bar shows how much of each voice is in the mix. Only the proportions matter: two voices at the same setting are always a 50/50 mix.

Tips

  • Two or three voices is plenty.
  • Stick to one accent. Mixing American and British voices tends to sound uncertain.

Write your script

Type or paste what you want spoken. Each line becomes its own piece.

One line, one piece

Every line is spoken separately with a pause after it. That means you can fix one line and re-create only that line, without touching the rest.

Punctuation is your steering wheel

  • A comma is a short breath. A colon builds a little anticipation.
  • A short line on its own reads as a heading and gets a longer pause.
  • Capital letters don't add emphasis. Rewording does.

A word comes out wrong?

Spell it the way it sounds ("sequel" for SQL), or add it to overrides.json once and every script gets the fix.

Listen and save

Create the audio, listen, adjust, and save the take you like.

Create audio

Speaks the whole script. To redo part of it, select those lines in the script and use Create audio for selected lines.

Save this take

Saves the audio as a WAV file, together with the exact settings and text that made it, in a folder named after your take. You can always recreate it later.

    Welcome to Kokoro Studio

    Kokoro is a small, free voice model that runs entirely on this computer. Nothing you type leaves your machine, and once the model is downloaded you don't need the internet.

    Your first recording, in four steps

    1. Pick a voice in step 1. The default blend is a good place to start.
    2. Paste your script in step 2, one sentence or idea per line.
    3. Create audio and listen.
    4. Happy with it? Name it and Save this take.

    Making it sound better

    • Too fast or too slow? Move Speaking speed.
    • Lines feel rushed together? Open More controls and lengthen the pauses.
    • One line sounds wrong? Reword it, select it, and create audio for just that line.

    Where your files go

    Saved takes go in the exports folder next to the studio. Each take has the audio, the settings, your script and the text Kokoro spoke.
